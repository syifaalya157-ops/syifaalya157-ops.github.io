Graduation photos in Padova

Graduating comes after years of work. The photos should show that day as it really was: you, the laurel wreath and the people who came with you.

We can take the photos on the day of your graduation, during the celebration with friends and family, or in a separate, calmer session a few days later.

We choose the places together: the squares of the city centre, Prato della Valle, your department, or a spot that means something to you.

Sessions in Padova from €60 for 1 hour and 40 edited photos. Student discounts available. See prices
