Couples and pre-wedding in Padova and Venice

A couple session is a chance to have photos of the two of you, taken calmly: before the wedding, for an anniversary, or just because.

In Venice it pays to start early in the morning, when the light is soft and the streets are quieter. Padova has arcades, squares and the Euganean Hills a short drive away.

If you're visiting from abroad, I'll help you choose places and times.

Sessions in Venice from €100 and in Padova from €60, for 1 hour and 40 edited photos. Discounts for young couples. See prices

How it works
